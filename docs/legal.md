# Legal: your rights to download

RomSeek is a download manager. Like Sonarr or Radarr, it is a tool: what you download with it is your responsibility.

## What RomSeek is, and isn't

- RomSeek **does not** host, include, link to or recommend any games, BIOS files, firmware, keys or download sites.
- RomSeek searches **indexers that you add yourself** in Prowlarr. The project doesn't endorse any indexer or tracker.
- Where a console maker gives firmware away for free (for example Sony's PS3 and PS Vita system software), RomSeek links to the **official download page**.
- Emulator links go to each emulator's **official website**.

## Only download what you have the right to

Before you download anything, make sure you're allowed to. Generally that means one of these:

- **Your own dumps.** Games, BIOS and firmware you copied from cartridges, discs and consoles you own, using your own hardware. This is the cleanest way to build a library.
- **Freely distributed content.** Homebrew, public-domain games, and games or firmware the rights holder gives away.
- **Content you have a licence for**, where that licence allows the copy you're making.

Things to keep in mind:

- **Laws differ by country.** In some countries, even a backup copy of a game you own isn't allowed. Check the law where you live.
- **Owning a game doesn't automatically give you the right to download a copy of it** from someone else.
- **BIOS and system firmware are copyrighted** by the console maker, just like games.
- **Decryption keys:** in many countries (for example under the US DMCA and the EU Copyright Directive), breaking or getting around copy protection is illegal on its own, even for games you own. RomSeek doesn't help you get keys, and the project won't discuss how to.

This page is general information, **not legal advice**. If you're unsure, ask a lawyer where you live.

## Using this project's GitHub

Don't use Issues, Discussions or pull requests to share, request or link to:

- ROMs, ISOs or other game files
- BIOS files, firmware or keys
- download sites, trackers or indexers for them

Posts that do will be removed. Help with setting up RomSeek, its features and bugs is always welcome.
